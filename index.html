What we build

Hardware and software made to work together, so one team owns the whole system from the airframe to the flight logs.

Inspection drones

Multirotor drones that fly close to bridges, towers and roofs and bring back clear images and 3D models.

  • Planned flights with obstacle avoidance
  • Camera, thermal and lidar payloads
  • Works with or without GPS

Ground robots

Wheeled robots for sites where a person should not have to walk every day, like solar farms, plants and campuses.

  • Repeatable patrol routes
  • Sensor fusion for rough terrain
  • Remote takeover at any time

Autonomy software

The planning, control and perception stack that runs on our robots, also available for your own vehicles.

  • Path planning and tracking control
  • Multi-drone missions
  • Simulation before every field test

How a project runs

Every robot is tested in simulation and in the field before it goes to work for you.

Scope

We visit the site, learn the job, and agree on what the robot must measure or deliver.

Simulate

We model the vehicle and the site, then tune the controller and mission plan on a computer first.

Field test

We fly or drive the real system at your site with your team, and adjust from real data.

Deploy

You get the robot, the software, training and support, with logs from every mission.
